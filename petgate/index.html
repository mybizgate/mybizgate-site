Welcome · Selamat datang

Not a cage. A cosy little hotel room, built for cats.

PetGate is a dedicated cat-only hotel in Kampung Pandan, Kuala Lumpur. Every guest gets a private rubberwood suite with mesh ventilation, a resting shelf, fresh water and their own litter tray, plus time in the bright play hall with cat trees, tunnels and an exercise wheel.

Whether you are away for a weekend, travelling for balik kampung, or just need a trusted groomer, your cat is cared for by people who genuinely love them.

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring at check-in?

Your cat's usual food (enough for the stay plus a day), their vaccination card, and anything that smells like home, such as a small blanket or toy. We provide litter, bowls, water dispensers and bedding.

Does my cat need to be vaccinated?

Yes. For the safety of every guest we ask that cats are up to date on their core vaccinations and free from fleas. Please mention any medical conditions in the notes when booking.

Can two cats from the same home share a suite?

Bonded cats can usually share a suite or be placed in adjoining suites. Let us know when you book and we will recommend the best arrangement.

Will my cat get time outside the suite?

Yes. Every guest has supervised free-roam time in the play hall, with cat trees, tunnels, scratchers and the exercise wheel. Shy cats are given their own quiet sessions.

Can I get updates while I am away?

Of course. Just ask in the WhatsApp chat and we will send a photo or short video of your cat.

What are the drop-off and pick-up times?

We arrange drop-off and pick-up by appointment on WhatsApp so there is always someone ready to welcome your cat. Tell us your preferred time when you book.

Do you groom cats that are not staying at the hotel?

Yes. Grooming is open to walk-in guests by appointment. Our groomer is a certified professional cat groomer and is experienced with nervous and long-haired cats.

How do I pay?

Payment is made at the hotel on check-in or pick-up. We will confirm the total on WhatsApp before your arrival so there are no surprises.
